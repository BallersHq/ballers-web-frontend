FAQ

Questions before kick-off?

Can't find your answer? Email support@ballershq.com.

When and where are you launching?

We're launching in Abuja on 1 October 2026, with more Nigerian cities to follow. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you the moment the app is live.

What happens if the arena doesn't confirm my booking?

Your payment is held until the arena confirms. If the arena declines, you're refunded in full and we can suggest another slot. If they don't respond in time, our team steps in to sort it out.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel 24 hours or more before your session and you get the full court fee back. Cancel later than that and you get 50% of the court fee back. Once the session has started, there's no refund. The small platform fee isn't refundable when you cancel, but if the arena cancels or declines, you get everything back.

Can I make money from my regular slot?

Yes. Claim the slot your squad already pays for, and once the arena confirms it's yours you can open a set number of spots to the public at your own price. Players pay upfront, and the money is released to your wallet after the game. Paid spots aren't refunded if someone doesn't show up.

How do I get money out of my wallet?

Withdraw to your Nigerian bank account from the app. Withdrawals start from ₦1,000, have no fee, and usually arrive in 1–3 business days. Every withdrawal needs your wallet PIN.

I own an arena. What does it cost to list?

It's free to join the waitlist and set up your arena. Before you take your first booking, we'll walk you through our pricing so you know exactly what you'll earn on every slot.
