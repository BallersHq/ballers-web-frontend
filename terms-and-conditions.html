02Key terms

  • Player: anyone who books a court, joins a game, contributes to a group fund or otherwise uses BallersHQ to play.
  • Host: a player who creates a game and invites others to join it, for free or for a price per spot.
  • Arena owner: a business or person who lists an arena and its pitches or courts on BallersHQ.
  • Booking: a reservation of a pitch or court for a specific date and time.
  • Wallet: your BallersHQ balance, used to pay for bookings, receive refunds and earnings, send money to other users and withdraw to your bank.
  • Platform fee: the fee BallersHQ charges for a booking, shown to you before you pay.

  • You are responsible for everything that happens on your account. Keep your password and transaction PIN private.

05What BallersHQ does

BallersHQ is a marketplace. We help players discover and book arenas, organise games and pay safely, and we help arena owners manage bookings and receive payments.

Arenas listed on BallersHQ are independently owned and operated. BallersHQ does not own or run the venues, and we are not responsible for the condition of facilities, equipment or the conduct of venue staff, although we verify arenas before they go live and step in when something goes wrong with a booking.

06Bookings and arena confirmation

  • When you book, you pay the court price, any add-ons you choose and the platform fee. All prices are shown in Naira before you confirm.
  • Some pitches confirm bookings instantly. For others, your payment is held safely and the arena has a limited time to confirm or decline the slot.
  • If the arena declines, you receive a full refund, including the platform fee, and we may suggest another slot.
  • If the arena doesn't respond in time, our team reviews the booking and either confirms it with the arena or refunds you in full.
  • Once your booking is confirmed you receive a booking reference and a QR ticket. Show the QR ticket at the arena to check in.
  • Please arrive on time. Your booking is for the time slot you paid for, and arenas are not required to extend it.

07Payments, fees and your wallet

  • Card and bank payments are processed by our payment partner, Flutterwave. By paying, you authorise the charge to your chosen payment method.
  • You can top up your wallet, pay for bookings from it, send money to other BallersHQ users and withdraw to a Nigerian bank account in your name.
  • Withdrawals start from ₦1,000, can be up to ₦5,000,000 per withdrawal, currently have no BallersHQ fee, and usually arrive within 1–3 business days.
  • Money that is held for a pending booking, game or group fund appears in your wallet but can't be withdrawn until it is released.
  • We may place a temporary hold on a wallet, delay a withdrawal or reverse a transaction if we reasonably suspect fraud, an error or a breach of these Terms.
  • You are responsible for any taxes that apply to money you earn through BallersHQ.

08Cancellations and refunds

How much you get back depends on who cancels and when. The full rules, including cancellations, paid game spots and group funds, are in our Refund Policy, which forms part of these Terms. In short, if you cancel 24 hours or more before your session you get the full court fee back, less than 24 hours before you get 50% of the court fee back, and after the session starts there is no refund. If the arena cancels or declines, you get everything back.

09Hosting and joining games

  • Hosts can keep a game private, invite-only or open to the public, and can set a price per spot or make it free.
  • When you pay to join a game, your payment is held and released to the host after the game has been played.
  • If you leave a paid game before it starts, you receive a refund. If you leave after it starts or don't show up, your spot is not refunded.
  • If the host cancels a game, everyone who paid is refunded.
  • Hosts are responsible for the games they organise, including describing them accurately and turning up.

10Squad chip-in (group funds)

  • An organiser can split the cost of a booking into shares and share an invite link. Anyone with the link can pay their share, pay for more than one spot or cover the rest of the bill.
  • The booking is confirmed once the full amount has been paid. If the target isn't reached by the deadline, the organiser cancels, or the slot is no longer available, everyone who contributed is refunded to their wallet.
  • You can withdraw your contribution while the fund is still open. Once the fund has completed, refunds are only available through a refund request that we review.
  • If you pay for other people, any repayment between you is your own arrangement.

11Regular slots

  • If you already pay an arena for a regular slot, you can claim it on BallersHQ. The arena must approve your claim before the slot is linked to your account.
  • You can then keep the slot private, invite people or open a set number of spots to the public at a price you choose. The rules for hosting games apply to spots you sell.
  • Your arrangement with the arena for the slot itself, including how and when you pay the arena, stays between you and the arena unless it is booked and paid for through BallersHQ.

12Arena owners

  • Keep your arena details, prices, opening hours, photos and availability accurate and up to date.
  • Respond to booking requests within the confirmation window and honour every booking you confirm. Repeatedly declining or cancelling confirmed bookings may lead to your arena being hidden or removed.
  • Provide a safe venue that follows applicable laws and safety standards.
  • We may ask for business registration details and documents to verify your arena, and we may pause payouts until verification is complete.
  • Earnings from a booking are released to your wallet after the player checks in with their QR ticket, or automatically after the session if there is no open dispute. BallersHQ's commission and payout terms will be agreed with you before you take your first booking.
  • Promotions you create are applied automatically at checkout and must be honoured.
